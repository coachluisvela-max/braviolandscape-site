Commercial landscape installation

BRAVÍO installs the landscape on new and renovated commercial sites in the Rio Grande Valley: trees, palms, shrubs, beds, sod and mulch, built to the plan under one contract.

Request a quote(956) 322-2640

Where we fit

Retail pads, banks, medical offices, restaurants, apartment communities and entries. Sites where the landscape has to be in, alive and accepted before the doors open.

What's included

  • Layout and staking for review before planting
  • Bed preparation and planting soil
  • Trees, palms, shrubs and groundcovers furnished and planted to the plant schedule
  • Sod or seed
  • Mulch
  • Tree staking and palm bracing as detailed
  • Watering and care until acceptance
  • Cleanup, written care instructions and a written warranty

For general contractors

Send the landscape sheets and your bid date. You get a firm written price before bid day, held for 90 days, with unit prices for additions and deductions and a clear list of what is and is not included. If the quantities on the plan do not add up, we tell you before you bid, not after.

Irrigation

Texas requires a licensed irrigator to install irrigation. We schedule our planting around your irrigator and tell them what each plant needs. Ask us about irrigation when you send the plans.

How the work is done

BRAVÍO holds the contract and the schedule. The field work is done by vetted, insured specialty crews, and we check it against the plan before asking you to accept it. Extra work happens only on a written change order.

What we need to quote

The landscape sheets and plant schedule, the site address and your dates. No plans? Tell us sizes and quantities, or send photos of the site.
