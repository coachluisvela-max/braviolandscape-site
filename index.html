Palm installation

Road widening, utility work or a redesign in the way? We dig mature palms, move them, and replant them on site or across town.

How a job runs

  1. Quote. You send sizes, quantities, the site address and your dates. We send a firm price.
  2. Tag. Your palms are selected and held in the field.
  3. Dig and deliver. Trees are dug close to planting day and trucked straight to the site.
  4. Install. We set, backfill, brace and water in each palm.
  5. Hand off. We walk the finished job with you and leave written care instructions and a one-year written warranty.
