Common questions

What does "clear trunk" mean?
Clear trunk is the height from the ground to the base of the lowest fronds. It is how palms are sized on commercial plans. Overall height includes the crown and runs several feet taller.
How big are the Sabals you supply?
From under 6 feet up to 15 feet and more of clear trunk, field-grown in the Rio Grande Valley.
Do you relocate existing palms?
Yes. We dig, move and replant mature palms for road projects, utility work and redevelopment.
Is there a warranty?
Every installation comes with a written warranty: one year where the site has working irrigation. Relocated palms carry a workmanship warranty.
Where do you work?
Across the Rio Grande Valley, from McAllen and Edinburg to Harlingen, Brownsville and South Padre Island, and in Corpus Christi, San Antonio and Houston for larger projects.
How do I get a price?
Send palm sizes, quantities, the site location and your dates, any hour of the day. We return a firm written quote. Contractors bidding a job can send the plant schedule and bid date.
When is the best time to plant palms in South Texas?
Palms establish fastest in warm soil, so the main season runs from spring through early fall.
