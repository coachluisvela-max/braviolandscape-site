Clear trunk and overall height are not the same

Clear trunk is measured from the ground to the base of the lowest fronds. Overall height includes the crown, which adds several feet. A palm that is 12 feet overall may have only 5 or 6 feet of clear trunk, and it is a much smaller, less expensive tree than one with 12 feet of clear trunk. Plans that say only "12 ft" get bid both ways.

How to specify Texas Sabals

  • Name the species: Texas Sabal, Sabal mexicana
  • State the size as clear trunk (CT), for example 12 ft CT
  • State the root ball: balled and burlapped (B&B), field grown
  • Say whether heights must be matched, and within what tolerance
  • Say whether trunks are to be smooth or with leaf bases left on

A sample plant schedule line

Sabal mexicana, Texas Sabal, 12 ft CT, B&B, field grown, matched, straight trunk.
