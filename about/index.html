About BRAVÍO

BRAVÍO Commercial Landscape was founded in McAllen in 2026 to do one thing well: put big Texas Sabals in the ground on commercial projects, and keep them alive.

Request a quote(956) 322-2640

Who is behind it

BRAVÍO was founded by Luis Vela, who spent five years directing operations for a South Texas palm grower before starting the company. That background is why BRAVÍO starts from the tree. How a palm is dug, how long it sits, and how it is set decide whether it is alive a year later.

How we work

  • A firm written quote before any work starts
  • Palms tagged in the field for your job and dug close to planting day
  • The owner on site for every installation
  • A walk-through at the end, with written care instructions and a written warranty
  • A change order in writing before any extra work, so the final bill holds no surprises

Where we are today

BRAVÍO is a new company, and our first installations begin in spring 2027. We would rather tell you that than borrow someone else's project photos. What we bring from day one is the supply of large field-grown Sabals and the knowledge of how to move them.

The name

Bravío is Spanish for fierce and untamed. It suits a palm that is native to the Rio Grande Valley and stands through South Texas heat and wind.
