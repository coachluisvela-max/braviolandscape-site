South Texas, by project

For projects of about 20 palms or more we travel to Corpus Christi, Laredo, San Antonio and the Houston and Galveston area. The palms are trucked from the Valley and planted by our crew.

Planting season

Palms establish fastest in warm soil. Our main installation season runs from spring through early fall. We quote year-round, and bids for spring work are best priced over the winter.
