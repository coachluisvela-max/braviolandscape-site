Why the Texas Sabal

The Texas Sabal (Sabal mexicana) is the Rio Grande Valley's own native palm. Once established it handles South Texas heat, wind and dry spells, and its full fan crown reads as a mature tree from the day it is planted. It grows slowly, which is why large specimens are worth sourcing from a grower who has them in the field.
